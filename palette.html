Thank you for taking part in this Editability Study. The project measures how the structure of a CAD model affects how easy it is to edit, following the usability study approach in Usability Engineering by Nielsen.

While recording, this logger saves: which commands you use, what kind of thing you select and its geometry (size and position), which design environment you are in, and the time of each action.

It does not save: your name or Autodesk account, file names or paths, feature names, typed values, or anything outside Fusion.

After each file, the panel asks you two short questions about it. Your answers are saved as you type them, so only write what you are happy to have recorded.

Logs are identified only by your participant code. A red banner in this panel blinks for as long as recording is on. You can stop at any time. Full details are in your participant information sheet.
